Even though our crew management agency has an SIREs certificate, shipowners who have a ship registered and flagged in a nation that has ratified MLC 2006 are required to use the services of our agency that satisfies the MLC 2006 minimum requirements.

When choosing and sending seafarers from a crewing agency based in a nation that has not ratified MLC 2006, flag states and shipowners should carefully investigate whether the agency complies with MLC 2006 standards.
